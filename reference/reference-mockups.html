Pawns of Olympus: reference mockups

These are rough sketches from the planning chat that show the chosen direction: GBA-era pixel art built from flat geometric shapes, with a Greek pottery palette. Treat them as a starting point, not the quality bar. The real game should be clearly better: cleaner sprites, more ground detail, animation and juice. Keep the same shape language and palette.

1. Battle scene (internal res 192x128, shown at 5x)

Achilles is selected (gold border) with his move range shown in light tiles. Ajax holds a tower shield. Two Trojans stand on red threat tiles, and a dotted gold line marks a planned attack. The pips above each unit show health. In the background are the walls of Troy, built from blocks.

2. Hero close-ups (Ajax, Achilles, Trojan)

Bodies are inverted triangles (Geometric-period vase figures), with half-circle helmet crests and round or tower shields. Each has a 1px dark outline, and a few light pixels as highlights on the bronze.

3. Gods (portraits, 48x56 each, shown at 4x)

Gods use hourglass bodies made of two triangles, long robes with a gold meander hem, and one signature item each. Their halos are dithered rings, not soft glow. Top row: Zeus (neutral), Athena and Poseidon (favor the Greeks). Bottom row: Apollo, Ares and Aphrodite (favor the Trojans).
